SDS Privacy Policy

Effective: September 16, 2026
Last updated: September 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MeierWerks Inc. ("MeierWerks," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, discloses, retains, and protects personal information when you use SDS (Speaker Design Suite) for macOS or iOS, its account and sync services, MW-Assist, and physical-hardware checkout (collectively, the "Service").

1. WHO WE ARE

SDS is provided by MeierWerks Inc., Kent, Connecticut 06757, United States.

Privacy questions or requests: privacy@meierwerks.com
Customer support: support@meierwerks.com
Website: https://meierwerks.com

For EEA, UK, and Swiss data-protection purposes, MeierWerks Inc. is the controller of personal information described in this Policy unless stated otherwise.

2. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

Account and profile information. We collect your email address, account identifier, display name, optional avatar, sign-in provider identifiers, account status, accepted policy versions, consent choices, and subscription or entitlement status. Authentication credentials are handled by our authentication provider. We do not receive your plaintext password.

Project and design information. Full project files remain on your device unless you choose a feature that transmits specified material. If you save or sync a project, use MW-Assist or another disclosed AI-assisted feature, or send project material to support, we process the loudspeaker, driver, crossover, enclosure, schematic, simulation, cabinet, and related project data and files needed for that feature. The Terms describe the different license rules for Non-Commercial Plan and Commercial License projects.

Measurement information. We process acoustic measurements, frequency-response data, impulse responses, calibration files and status, microphone and device-model metadata, and related files that you create, import, save, or sync.

MW-Assist and AI-assisted feature information. When you choose to use MW-Assist or another disclosed AI-assisted feature, the Service sends the text you enter and relevant conversation, project, workspace, datasheet, or image context through our authenticated server to our AI service provider, currently Anthropic, so the requested response or analysis can be generated. The app discloses this transfer and asks for your permission before an AI-assisted feature sends anything for the first time, and you can turn MW-Assist off at any time from the assistant's options menu. Do not submit information that you are not authorized to share.

Digital-purchase information. Apple processes payment for subscriptions and digital add-ons purchased through the App Store. We receive transaction identifiers, product identifiers, subscription status, expiration or revocation status, and related receipt or entitlement information needed to unlock and reconcile digital access. We do not receive your full payment-card details from Apple.

Physical-order information. Stripe processes payment for shipped physical hardware. We receive order items, amounts, currency, payment and fulfillment status, contact details, shipping address, taxes, shipping method, tracking information, refunds, disputes, and supplier references needed to fulfill and support the order. Stripe, not MeierWerks, receives and processes your full card number and security code.

Support and communications. We collect information you send when you contact us, submit a privacy request, or report a problem. Support diagnostics may include app and operating-system versions, device type, selected workspace, sync state, and redacted error information. The support flow is designed to exclude credentials, API keys, authentication tokens, full project contents, and account identifiers from diagnostics.

Device and installation information. To enforce the per-account device limit, the app creates a random installation identifier on first launch, stores it on your device, and sends it to our server with your device class (computer or phone) and the name your operating system reports for the device (for example, the computer name shown in your macOS settings) when it registers or renews an account device lease. This identifier is app-private: it is not a hardware serial number or advertising identifier, it is linked to your account only while a lease exists, and it is not used for tracking. You can see your active devices and end their sessions from Settings ▸ Account.

Security and service information. Our providers and systems may process IP address, device and operating-system information, app version, sign-in and session events, request timestamps, service logs, crash or error information, fraud signals, and security events needed to operate and protect the Service.

Preference and policy records. We retain your marketing preference and records of policy disclosures and acceptance, together with the applicable policy version and timestamp.

Local engineering samples. On eligible Non-Commercial Plans, the current app may create an install-scoped local queue of numeric, de-identified engineering samples such as enclosure count, driver-way count, and alignment category. The current release does not upload that queue. It excludes user ID, project name, file path, free text, and full project contents. Engineering-data collection is off for the Commercial tier's offline/no-cloud mode. We will update this Policy and the app disclosure and obtain any permission required by law or Apple's rules before enabling remote collection.

We use personal information to:

- create, authenticate, secure, and support your account;
- provide project storage and cross-device sync;
- provide measurements, simulations, MW-Assist, and other requested features;
- verify StoreKit purchases and maintain digital entitlements;
- price, process, fulfill, support, refund, and account for physical orders;
- communicate service, security, purchase, support, and policy notices;
- detect errors, fraud, abuse, and security incidents;
- comply with tax, accounting, legal, and regulatory obligations;
- improve reliability, accessibility, safety, and product quality; and
- send marketing only as permitted by your marketing preference and applicable law, and process service, product, analytics, and product-improvement data as described in this Policy and the Terms.

If we lawfully receive Project Materials covered by the Non-Commercial Plan license in the Terms, that license permits us to analyze and use them to research, develop, test, train or evaluate systems, manufacture, market, license, and monetize current or future products and services. Our contractual right to use material is separate from whether the current app technically transmits it. Identifiable personal information is used only as disclosed here, as permitted by your settings and applicable law. Commercial License Project Materials are excluded unless you separately and affirmatively authorize their use.

We may create, retain, use, disclose, license, and monetize aggregated or de-identified engineering datasets, models, benchmarks, patterns, and product improvements that are not reasonably linkable to you. We maintain safeguards designed to prevent reidentification and do not attempt to reidentify that data except to test and maintain those safeguards or as otherwise permitted by law.

For EEA and UK users, our legal bases are performance of a contract, compliance with legal obligations, our legitimate interests in operating and securing the Service, and consent where consent is required. You may withdraw consent at any time without affecting earlier lawful processing.

4. HOW WE DISCLOSE INFORMATION

We do not sell personal information. We do not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising, and the app does not use third-party advertising trackers.

We may sell or license products, services, designs, models, benchmarks, or datasets developed from Project Materials as permitted by the Terms, or from aggregated or de-identified information. We do not treat the sale or license of genuinely de-identified material as a sale of personal information, and we do not sell an identifiable user's raw project file or personal information.

We disclose information only as reasonably necessary to:

- Supabase, for authentication, database, storage, Edge Functions, and account sync;
- Apple, for App Store distribution, Sign in with Apple, StoreKit billing, and transaction verification;
- Google, if you choose Google sign-in;
- Anthropic, when you choose MW-Assist or an AI-assisted feature;
- Stripe, for physical-goods payment processing, tax, fraud controls, and receipts;
- component suppliers, carriers, and fulfillment providers, including Parts Express where applicable, for availability, purchasing, shipment, returns, and order support;
- support, security, diagnostics, hosting, email, and professional advisers acting for us; and
- courts, regulators, law enforcement, or other parties when required by law or reasonably necessary to protect rights, safety, security, or the integrity of the Service.

If MeierWerks is involved in a merger, financing, acquisition, reorganization, bankruptcy, or sale of assets, information may be transferred as part of that transaction subject to applicable law.

5. RETENTION

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above:

- account, synced project, and measurement data: while your account is active, then deleted or de-identified within 30 days after we verify and begin an account-deletion request, except as described below;
- service backups: removed through the normal backup cycle, ordinarily within 90 days after deletion from active systems;
- physical-order, payment, tax, and accounting records: generally seven years after the transaction, or longer if law requires;
- support and security records: generally up to 24 months, unless a longer period is needed for an active dispute, fraud investigation, security incident, or legal hold;
- consent and policy-acceptance records: while the account is active and as needed to demonstrate compliance; and
- AI usage and entitlement records: as needed to provide, meter, secure, and audit the feature. AI inputs may also be processed or retained by the AI provider under the applicable service configuration and legal requirements.

Aggregated or de-identified datasets, models, benchmarks, know-how, product improvements, and other results that are not reasonably linkable to you may be retained indefinitely. Deleting an account does not require MeierWerks to withdraw a product, model, dataset, analysis, or other non-identifying result already created in accordance with the Terms and this Policy.

Deleting the app does not delete cloud data. Use Settings > Account > Delete account to initiate deletion. We may retain limited information when required for taxes, payment disputes, fraud prevention, security, legal claims, or another legal obligation. Where feasible, retained information is isolated from ordinary product use.

7. ACCOUNT DELETION AND SUBSCRIPTIONS

You may initiate deletion from Settings on macOS or iOS. Deletion covers the account and associated personal information that we are not legally required to retain. If manual review is required, we will keep you informed and aim to complete verified requests within 30 days.

Deleting your SDS account does not automatically cancel an Apple subscription. Manage or cancel Apple billing at https://apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions. Where Sign in with Apple was used, we will revoke the associated Apple authorization as part of the completed deletion process where required.
