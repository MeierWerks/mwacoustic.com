SDS Support

DRAFT — Bennett approval required before publicationSDS for macOS, Windows and iOS

SDS is the speaker design suite from MeierWerks Inc. This page is the support contact for the app, for your MeierWerks account, and for orders placed from inside SDS.

Contact

Support and bug reports: support@meierwerks.com

Privacy requests (access, correction, deletion, objection): privacy@meierwerks.com

We answer from Kent, Connecticut, on U.S. Eastern business days. If an email concerns a Stripe hardware order, include the Stripe order or receipt reference. For an Apple digital purchase, include Apple's order or transaction information if available. Windows digital purchases are not offered in this release.

The fastest route is inside the app: Settings ▸ Support ▸ Email opens a support message, and Settings ▸ Support ▸ Report opens a bug report. Both prefill a short, redacted diagnostics summary that may include app and operating-system versions, device type, selected workspace, sync state, and redacted error information. The support flow is designed to exclude credentials, API keys, authentication tokens, full project contents, and account identifiers.

What to include in a report

  • SDS version. Read it from Settings ▸ Support ▸ Current build and paste it as shown; it identifies the exact build you are running.
  • Operating-system version. On Mac, use Apple menu ▸ About This Mac. On Windows, use Settings ▸ System ▸ About.
  • Steps to reproduce. What you did, what you expected, and what happened instead. One step per line is ideal.
  • Where in the app. The workspace (Design, Enclosure, Crossover, Simulate, Workshop, Cart) and the control you used.
  • Optional. The redacted diagnostics from Settings ▸ Support ▸ Copy diagnostics or Export diagnostics, and a screenshot. Only attach a project file if you are comfortable sharing its contents; we never need it to open a ticket.

Your account

Sign-in help

SDS signs in with email and a verification code, Sign in with Apple, or Google. If a verification code does not arrive within a few minutes, check your spam folder and use Resend code. If you are still stuck, email support from the address you signed up with and we will look at the account state.

Delete your account

Open Settings ▸ Account ▸ Delete account in SDS. Deletion removes your account and the associated personal information we are not legally required to keep; synced projects and measurements are deleted or de-identified within 30 days after the request is verified. You can also email privacy@meierwerks.com from your account address to request deletion.

Deleting your SDS account does not cancel an Apple subscription or a subscription billed through another merchant account. Manage or cancel Apple billing at apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions. Windows digital subscriptions are not offered at launch. If MeierWerks later offers a Windows subscription through Stripe, cancel its renewal through the subscription-management page identified in the receipt before deleting your account; after cancellation, deleting the account ends any remaining paid time without a refund. For a subscription billed through another merchant, billing continues until you cancel it through that merchant. Deleting the app from your computer does not delete cloud data.

Export your data

Settings ▸ Account lets you export an account snapshot. Project files are ordinary documents on your computer and never need an export.

Purchases, subscriptions and orders

Apple subscriptions and add-ons (digital) are sold by Apple through In-App Purchase. Apple handles billing, renewal, cancellation, Family Sharing and refunds. Manage a subscription at apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions; request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com. Use Restore purchases in the subscription screen if an entitlement is missing on a new Mac.

Windows subscriptions and add-ons (digital) are not offered at launch, and paid Windows checkout is unavailable in this release. If MeierWerks later offers Windows digital purchases through Stripe, the purchase flow and receipt will identify the merchant, price and renewal terms, subscription-management process, and refund route before purchase.

Hardware orders (physical) — drivers, crossover components and cabinet hardware bought from the SDS cart — are paid through Stripe on Stripe's own secure checkout form and may be sourced or fulfilled by third-party distributors, including Parts Express. Stripe emails your receipt; shipping and tracking come from the applicable fulfillment provider. Contact support@meierwerks.com within 30 days of delivery for a return authorization on an unused stock item or to report a missing, damaged or incorrect item. Do not ship a return without an authorization.

MeierWerks never sees your full card number for Apple digital purchases or Stripe hardware orders; Apple and Stripe process those payment details. Windows digital purchases are not offered in this release. The cabinet build shown in the cart is informational and is not charged; only the ticked hardware lines are purchased.

Privacy and legal

Windows users: read the Windows Privacy Policy and Windows Terms of Use (version 7). Apple-platform users: read the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use. MW-Assist sends your request and the relevant project context to our AI provider only after you allow it, and can be turned off from the assistant's options menu. Third-party software notices are available in Settings.

Known issues and release notes

Known issues are tracked for every release candidate and reviewed before App Store submission or direct Windows publication; release notes are updated with each build and shown under Settings ▸ Support ▸ Release notes. If you hit something that is not listed there, please report it — a clear reproduction is the most useful thing you can send us.
